G-Budget-Builder was created for a simple reason:
"Budgeting shouldn't be stressful".

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at University of Witwatersrand, Johannesburg. I built this website simply because I was tired of complicated spreadsheets and apps that tell you to "STOP BUYING COFFEE".
You should be able to save 10%+ more this year without sacrificing ice cream

My Goal

To build an original piece of a tool that helps students and young
professionals in South Africa take control of their money in 10 minutes
a day. No finance degree needed.

Built With

  • HTML5 + CSS3
  • PHP + MySQL for backend
  • XAMPP for local development
